# Complex native-model exploration → mandatory review

Native general models may attempt hard math, mechanisms, counterexamples, or scheme exploration when the lead assigns a clear question, inputs, budget, stop conditions, and auditable artifacts.

Rules:
1. Label outputs **candidate / pending review**.
2. Author does not self-clear.
3. Review seat organizes verification; prefer a different capable channel (e.g. dedicated coding agent, planner UI, or independent implementation) for key reasoning, sources, and calculations.
4. Same model behind a different bot name ≠ independent model review.
5. Only after review may results enter formal conclusions or dependent follow-ons.
6. On fail: keep original artifacts and named issues; bounded revise within the original budget. Negative results may accept.
7. This rule does not auto-expand permissions or spend.
